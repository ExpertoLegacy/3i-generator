RESEARCH REPORT GENERATOR

No installation is needed.

1. Copy this whole folder to your computer, for example to Documents.
   Keep everything together. The app needs the _internal folder beside it.

2. Double-click ResearchReportGenerator.exe.
   The first start can take up to a minute. Later starts are faster.

3. On the first screen, choose "check this computer" to see whether anything needs attention.


IF WINDOWS SAYS IT PROTECTED YOUR PC

The app is not yet code-signed, so Windows does not recognize its publisher.
Choose "More info", then "Run anyway".

Do this only for a copy you received directly from your teacher.


IF YOUR ANTIVIRUS REMOVES THE APP

Do not turn your antivirus off. Tell your teacher, who can check the copy and report
the false alarm. Compare the file's fingerprint with SHA256.txt beside the ZIP file.


YOUR WORK

Each report is saved as one .rrg file wherever you choose. Keep that file safe.
Deleting this folder does not delete your reports.
The full guide is inside the app, under File, then Student guide.
