Student Guide

How to turn your research into your school's final report, from your first answer to the oral defense.

Install the app

  1. Run the installer. You do not need an administrator password.
  2. If Windows says it protected your PC, choose More info, then Run anyway. This appears because the app is not code-signed. Do this only for a copy from your teacher.
  3. You need no account. You need internet only for the AI tools and the online source search.
  4. Open Check this computer on the first screen. It tells you if anything needs attention.
  5. If your antivirus removes the app, ask your teacher to allow it. New apps that are not code-signed are sometimes flagged by mistake.
  6. On school computers that erase changes at restart, keep your report on a USB drive. You may need to install the app again after each restart.

Save your work

  • Choose Start a new report and pick where to save. Your whole report, with all files, is one .rrg file.
  • The app saves as you type. You never need a save button.
  • To share with your group or adviser, copy the .rrg file. Only one person should edit at a time, or one person's work will replace the other's.
  • Working from a USB drive is fine. The top bar tells you when your work has reached the drive. Wait for it to say so before you remove the drive.
  • Anyone with the file can read it, including consent forms and photos. Keep it on devices you trust, and delete it when you no longer need it.

Work through the tabs

  1. Project Setup first. These answers fill in your report everywhere.
  2. Do the chapters in order. Each part opens after you approve the one before it, and each chapter opens after the last chapter is done. If you later revise an approved part, approve it again.
  3. Chapter 1: the app makes a first draft from your Project Setup and marks what it does not know with [KULANG: ...]. Answer its questions in Tagalog, Taglish, or English. The app writes formal academic English.
  4. Chapter 2: approve the recommended topics. The app then finds real published studies, adds them to your Source Library, and writes the draft. Open and read the studies, because the draft is based only on their abstracts. Remove any that do not fit. Try the actual systems before you approve Related Systems.
  5. Optional parts have a Not necessary button. You can bring them back at any time.
  6. Chapter 3: choose from the suggested research designs and sampling methods, and the app writes them up. Locale and Population come from you. After you approve the whole chapter, download the research instrument and use it to collect your data.
  7. Chapter 4: paste your GitHub link or choose your project folder, and the app describes your actual system. It never opens secret files such as .env. For results, enter only your real summarized data. The app writes the discussion from it and never guesses results.
  8. Chapter 5, References, and Appendices: the app drafts them from your approved chapters. Appendices you do not need can be marked Not necessary. Signed forms and photos must still be your real files.
  9. You make the final decision. Edit each draft, then choose Approve and continue.
  10. Build each chapter. When all its parts are approved, choose Build under the part list. This adds the chapter to your Word master, saved beside your report file, and updates the preview on the right.
  11. A dot beside each part shows its state. A half-filled dot means in progress, yellow means built but not reviewed, and green means reviewed.

Use your real evidence

  • Evaluation results: on Acceptability, import your CSV or Excel responses, such as a Google Forms export. The app computes the weighted means and the test of difference. Names and emails are not kept.
  • Reliability: on Research Instrument, import your pilot responses to compute Cronbach's alpha.
  • Photos and scans: add them on their pages. They stay inside your report file. Get consent before including photos of people.
  • Diagrams: fill in the table and choose Draw diagram. This works without internet. If you change the table, draw again.

Use AI honestly

  • Draft with AI turns your own answers into formal paragraphs. AI needs your real information first, so results parts stay locked until your data exists.
  • Check with AI reviews a part like an adviser. Suggest ideas helps with planning fields.
  • AI receives your answers with names, emails, and phone numbers replaced. Your files and photos are never sent.
  • You remain responsible for every line. AI can change the meaning of a sentence without any warning. Compare every sentence with your answers before you mark a part as reviewed.
  • If the app lists an invented citation or a number that is not in your data, correct it. Never leave it in because it sounds good.
  • Screenshots, photos, results, letters, and signatures must be real. AI images are only for a logo idea, a labeled concept mockup, and a slide background.
  • AI has a shared daily allowance that resets at 8:00 AM Philippine time. The top bar shows about how many tasks are left. Use it for what matters.
  • On the Ethics page, read your AI-use statement and confirm it. If you used an AI tool outside this app, disclose it there and in Phase 3.

Get your adviser's feedback

  • Give your adviser a copy of your .rrg file. Notes are added under each part, and a red number shows where they are.
  • Address each note, mark it as resolved, and take the updated file back.

Generate your report

  1. Open Finish and Consistency Check, then fix what it lists.
  2. A draft report can be generated anytime and is marked DRAFT on each page.
  3. A final report needs every part reviewed, every adviser note resolved, your AI-use statement confirmed, every citation in your Source Library, and your diagrams up to date.
  4. When Word opens the report, choose Yes to update the table of contents and page numbers.

Prepare for your defense

Open Finish and Defense Prep for a slide outline and likely panel questions. Write the answers in your own words. Every member must be able to explain the whole paper and the code.

If something goes wrong

  • AI says it needs internet: connect and wait a minute. Everything else keeps working. Check this computer shows the reason, such as a wrong date and time or a blocked school network.
  • Word will not save: close the report in Word, then try again.
  • A report will not open or is damaged: the app offers its most recent safety copy. It keeps the last three.
  • You replaced text by mistake: choose Restore previous version on that part.
  • You still need help: give your adviser the file app.log from %LOCALAPPDATA%\ResearchReportGenerator. It records only how the app ran, not what you wrote.
